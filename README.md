# Rapid Agile Web Development Sprint: Student Volunteer Network

> **Role:** Scrum Master  
> **Cohort Context:** 1 of 7 Scrum Masters facilitating autonomous student dev teams across a cohort-wide simulation  
> **Simulation Format:** 2.5-Hour Timeboxed In-Class Lab (Three 35-Minute "Scrum-Lite" Sprints) 
> **Deliverables:** Production Web Platform & Sprint Retrospective Deck 
> **Live Site:** [Student Volunteer Network](https://tomleha2004.wixsite.com/student-volunteer-ne)

---

## Executive Overview

Served as Scrum Master for a cross-functional development team during an intensive 2.5-hour Agile simulation. The objective was to build and launch the **Student Volunteer Network**—a live, responsive platform connecting university students with vetted, career-building volunteer roles. 

Across the cohort of 7 Scrum Masters, the Product Owner (Course Professor) introduced progressive constraint reveals at each review cycle. Governed by strict Scrum Master boundaries, zero hands-on editing was permitted (no touching the Wix editor, visual layouts, or slide deck). The role focused entirely on servant leadership: running timeboxed micro-ceremonies, clarifying incoming PO requirements, and coaching the team through high-velocity pivots under tight delivery deadlines.

---

## Sprint Cycles & Dynamic Stakeholder Pivots

The lab followed a strict **"Scrum-lite" framework** across **three 35-minute sprints** (5 min Planning, 25 min Active Build, 5 min PO Review/Retro):

### Sprint 1: Baseline Paper Specifications
* **Initial Briefing:** The Product Owner issued written paper instructions outlining the foundational project scope: an attractive desktop/mobile landing page, at least three partner organizations, five total volunteer opportunities, and official contact/submission channels.
* **Execution & Delivery:** Guided backlog prioritization and self-organizing task distribution. The team established core site architecture, navigation hubs, and baseline listings on Wix.

### Sprint 2: Progressive Constraint Pivot (Non-Religious & Non-Political)
* **PO Milestone Review:** Meeting with the 7 Scrum Masters, the Product Owner delivered an unannounced policy filter: **partner organizations and individual volunteer opportunities could carry zero religious or political affiliations**.
* **Scrum Master Intervention & Audit:** Filtered the new constraint to the development team, initiated an immediate backlog audit, and verified that existing site profiles fully complied without needing a structural rebuild.

### Sprint 3: The Critical Pivot (Direct Non-Profit Sourcing vs. Aggregators)
* **PO Milestone Review:** At the final review, the Product Owner revealed a strict sourcing constraint: **volunteer roles could not be sourced through third-party aggregators—all opportunities had to originate directly from the host non-profit**.
* **The Crisis:** Auditing the current directory showed that only 1 of the 3 chosen organizations satisfied this standard, disqualifying 66% of the catalog right before final deployment.
* **Rapid Recovery Execution:** Facilitated an emergency re-planning session to reallocate dev bandwidth. Guided team members into focused sourcing and authoring pairs to rapidly identify, vet, and publish two compliant direct-host non-profits (*Atlanta Technology Professionals*, *Inspiredu*, and *Atlanta Community Food Bank*) before the 2.5-hour timer expired.

---

## Core Agile Retrospectives & Key Takeaways

* **Uncovering Implicit Constraints:** Static initial documentation rarely reveals every stakeholder expectation; continuous discovery during execution is critical for preventing late-stage rework.
* **Low-Cost Pivoting Under Time Pressure:** Timeboxed sprints insulated the project from collapse during the Sprint 3 aggregator pivot, transforming an unexpected rejection into an immediate recovery milestone.
* **Pure Servant Leadership:** By enforcing ceremonies, shielding developers from stakeholder panic, and facilitating rapid re-scoping without touching keyboard assets, process leadership directly drove successful delivery under hard deadlines.
